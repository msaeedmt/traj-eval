# Comparison Contract

The final comparison will report, by task and arm:

- external kernel-validated solve rate and statement preservation;
- silent failures, unsolved trials, infrastructure-unknown trials;
- worker, controller, and total model calls;
- output tokens when the provider reports usage, latency, and tool failures;
- repeated-call and failed-compile stuck terminations;
- Reasoner/planner stuck recovery, Engineer strategic replanning, and local
  Engineer repair;
- communication graph metrics.

Primary comparisons are paired by task and trial index. Results remain
descriptive for two deliberately selected weak tasks. The report must discuss
strengths and drawbacks and may claim burden or a promising local routing effect,
but not overall NLP-proposal improvement.
